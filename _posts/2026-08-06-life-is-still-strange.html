I used to fetishize the aesthetic of smoking while sad. Like every other 14-year-old anorexic-wannabe queer on Tumblr, I saw myself as the lovechild of Skins’ Effy Stonem and Cassie Ainsworth: a food-starved, acid-dropping rebel. Give me a cigarette, and stick me in a black-and-white GIF. I didn’t eat for three days so I could be lovely — and fuck strange DILFs off Grindr in their garages, all while blitzed on molly. I don’t think that was supposed to be in the script. Lovely.

It becomes less glamorous when you’re nearing 30. Suddenly, your biggest concerns are all different: tretinoin from Vanuatu (you’re a wrinkly hag), finishing your degree (you’ll definitely die destitute), and finding a husband (you’ll die alone, too). My knees hurt if I don’t do my morning floor stretches. I have a penchant for soluble fiber and peach kombucha (microbiomemaxxing). When I feel particularly fancy, I make homemade rejuvelac. Kombucha is for posers — fermented quinoa water is for true probiotic lovers. Suddenly, I can no longer justify spending $60 a week on Juul pods. Oh, wow.
