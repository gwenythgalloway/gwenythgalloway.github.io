--- title: 'St. Union Man' date: 2026-07-06 categories: poetry excerpt: "Keep baring me through bearing me." image: /assets/images/stunionman/rowhomes.png teaser: /assets/images/stunionman/rowhomes.png ---

Don't touch the meatballs
on the stove. Touch
the god damn AC. Don't
rush, though. Take your time.

Asshole.

Don't touch the table
right after I polish it. Don't
go near my rose bushes.
Don't trim yours, either.

Got it?

I don't ask for a lot. Just
your hands on my face. Just
your last name, and name
for my last breath.

Don't touch the dish soap. Don't
touch my shampoo. Don't
touch me when I say "don't". Don't
ever make me afraid to say "don't".

And you don't. You just
drag your dirty boots
on my clean carpet. Asshole,
I'll have your dick

Nailed to the door like Jesus

Christ, Himself, ordained us.
He blessed us at St. Joe's
in front of angels and
your demonic mother.

Don't take your hands
off my cold cream face.
Don't put lotion on
your fat soul-sanding fingers.

Keep baring me through bearing me.

Don't stop your smirking
with that slimy, cocky mug
when I flail my spoons at you,
my handsome union man.

Don't die before I do.
Don't touch my china.
Don't leave me alone.
Don't leave, honey.

* * *

Gwenyt Galloway is a poet and writer native to Philadelphia, Pensylvania. Her work explores topics including environmentalism, interpersonal relationships, and the rise of contemporary American fascism.
